Community Guidelines

Help keep OpenShelf welcoming, thoughtful, and focused on books.

Share the shelf respectfully

  • Be respectful. Discuss books and ideas civilly, even when opinions differ.
  • Write genuine reviews. Reviews and ratings should reflect your own experience with the book. Do not post fake, coordinated, or misleading ratings.
  • No spam. Avoid repetitive posts, unrelated promotions, or content intended to manipulate ratings or visibility.
  • No harassment or abuse. Threats, hate speech, targeted harassment, and abusive or discriminatory content are not allowed.
  • Use reviews for books. Keep feedback relevant to the book and mark significant plot spoilers where the site provides that option.

Enforcement

OpenShelf administrators may review and moderate content that violates these guidelines. Depending on the circumstances, content may be rejected or removed and an account may be suspended. Suspended accounts cannot log in, and their approved reviews are hidden from public listings and ratings while the suspension is active.

Do not use the site to threaten, harass, impersonate, or manipulate other readers. If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, contact the site administrator.
